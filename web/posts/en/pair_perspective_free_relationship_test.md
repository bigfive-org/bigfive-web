## How similarly do you and your partner experience your relationship?

Most couples have a general sense of how their relationship is going. You may feel close, supported, and understood—or you may know that there are a few topics you keep putting off. But it can be difficult to see which parts of the relationship you experience in the same way and which parts look different from each side.

[Pair Perspective](https://pairperspective.com) is a free relationship test for couples designed to make that comparison easier. You both answer independently, then receive a shared result that shows your areas of alignment, strengths, and differences worth exploring together.

## A relationship test that starts a conversation

Pair Perspective is not about finding a winner or producing a final verdict about your relationship. It is a conversation tool. The questions give you both a chance to reflect on the relationship from your own point of view, while the result gives you a concrete place to begin talking.

The assessment includes 57 short statements across 14 important areas of relationship life, including:

- Communication and feeling heard
- Security, commitment, and trust
- Warmth and responsiveness
- Conflict and emotional stability
- Sex and physical intimacy
- Stress, teamwork, and mental load
- Friendship, fun, and everyday joy
- Respect, freedom, boundaries, values, and the future

This broad range helps you look beyond a single overall relationship score. A couple can feel very connected while still having different experiences of stress, household responsibilities, intimacy, or plans for the future.

## How Pair Perspective works

Using the relationship test takes about 10–12 minutes for each person:

1. **Take the assessment.** Rate 57 statements from your own perspective.
2. **Invite your partner.** Share a private link so your partner can complete the questions separately.
3. **Explore the result together.** Once both of you are finished, compare your shared picture, topic scores, and individual answers.

Your partner cannot see your responses while answering. The answers are stored separately and the shared result becomes available only when you are both done. No account, payment, subscription, or complicated setup is required.

## What can you learn from a couples questionnaire?

A relationship test can help you notice things that are easy to miss in everyday life. You may discover that you both feel strongly supported, or that you have different ideas about what makes communication work. You may also find that one of you feels more affected by stress or carries more of the mental load than the other realises.

These differences are not automatically problems. They are invitations to be curious. Instead of asking who is right, you can ask: “What is this like for you?” and “What would help us here?”

The result highlights the largest differences first, making it easier to choose one topic at a time. It also shows the strengths that are worth recognising and protecting—not only the areas that need attention.

## Is Pair Perspective a scientific test or couples therapy?

Pair Perspective is a free self-reflection and conversation tool. It is not a clinical assessment, a diagnosis, couples therapy, or medical advice. There are no right or wrong answers, and the results should not be used to label either partner or make major decisions on their own.

The value is in the conversation that follows. Take the result as a starting point for listening, understanding each other, and noticing what you may want to discuss more openly.
